Who We Are

Dedicated to Educational & Academic Excellence

Nazir Ajmal Memorial College of Education (Boys' Campus), located in Hojai, Assam, is a premier higher education institution affiliated with Gauhati University. Established under the aegis of the Ajmal Group of Institutions, the campus strives to provide quality undergraduate education and teacher training programs.

Our dedicated faculty and modern campus infrastructure ensure that students develop strong academic foundations, leadership skills, and ethical values required to excel in competitive domains.

Our Institutional Vision

To emerge as a center of excellence in higher education and teacher training, empowering male scholars with intellectual capabilities, critical thinking skills, and social responsibility.

Our Core Mission

  • Provide rigorous academic training under Gauhati University curriculum.
  • Foster creative writing, research, and critical analysis.
  • Build disciplined, skilled professionals for regional and national growth.
