About NAMCE Boys Campus

Academic Excellence

Nazir Ajmal Memorial College of Education (NAMCE) is dedicated to delivering high-caliber educational standards, fostering critical inquiry across Political Science, Sociology, English, and Teacher Training disciplines.

Departmental Structure

Our campus supports structured 8-semester undergraduate and training degree paths. Students engage in continuous academic publishing, magazine editing, and digital content creation.

Faculty Leadership

Our faculty guides students through research methodologies, cultural event curation, and regular peer-reviewed contributions accessible through the dedicated Teacher Portal.
